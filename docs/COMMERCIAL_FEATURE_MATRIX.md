## Descriptions

- **BioNova Rural Clinic** — Offline-first Android workflows, trilingual, fast prescriptions and simple billing for single-doctor rural clinics.
- **Clinic Starter** — OPD, e-prescriptions, billing, pharmacy and basic reports for small clinics.
- **Clinic Plus + AI** — Adds laboratory, advanced analytics, WhatsApp and AI scribe / business copilot.
- **Multi-Specialty Clinic** — Multiple departments and branches with knowledge assistant and document AI.
- **Hospital Core** — IPD, beds, nursing, emergency and insurance/TPA workflows for hospitals.
- **Hospital Advanced** — Adds OT scheduling, AI agents and forecasting, ABDM and FHIR interoperability.
- **Enterprise** — Unlimited scale, custom roles, API access and priority support — contract-based.
